# EmeilSG — UI/UX and design quality audit, 2026-10-12

_Fifth report of the 2026-10-12 audit. Branch `feature-security-audit-fixes`, uncommitted. The changes made
here are in the ui lane and are listed in `UI_NOTES.md`._

## How it was done

The app was built and run against the local stack (database, sign-in and gateway only), and a script drove
Edge through **39 routes at three widths** — 1440, 820 and 390 pixels — signed out, as an Admin and as a
Project Manager: 117 screenshots per pass, three passes (before, after the first fixes, final). On every page
it recorded console errors and failed requests, whether the page was wider than the window and which element
caused it, the number of top-level headings, controls without an accessible name, and — on the landing page —
the keyboard tab order and focus indicators. Eight screenshots were looked at by eye; the rest were judged by
the measurements. The screenshots are in the session's temporary folder, not in the repository.

**Limits.** The local database is nearly empty, so tables, charts and queues were mostly seen in their empty
states. The Special and Dark themes, the side panels and dialogs when open, the verify screen with a real
document, and the report detail page were **not** exercised. No screen reader was used. Contrast was computed
from colour values (previous report), not measured on screen.

## Result

No console error or failed request on any route. Sign-in, the HttpOnly session cookie and sign-out work in a
real browser. The product is consistent in structure — one shell, one table pattern, one header pattern — and
the landing page has no fake testimonials, logos or counters. The defects were concentrated in two places:
**phone layouts** (14 page-and-width combinations scrolled sideways; one remains, by 3 pixels) and the **three
newest Admin pages** (Deadlines, Carbon credits, Simulator), which drift from the design system the rest of
the app follows.

## Page by page — what remains

### Landing (`/`)

- **Custom cursor.** The native cursor is hidden on every page, the app included, and replaced by a drawn
  one. The design rules of this audit ask for cursor effects to be removed; the project's own landing brief
  says the cursor is intended and must not be broken. **Left as it is — the owner's decision.** If it stays,
  limiting it to the landing page would spare the working screens.
- **Hero card deck.** Twelve capability cards are stacked and rotate; the cards behind show cut-off text
  ("…ping", "Verify, then approve" through the top card). Two card titles appear twice in the page outline
  ("Human-Verified Data", "Role-Based Access").
- **Figures on the cards.** "LINEAGE INTEGRITY — 100% Linked to Source PDF" and "Portfolio-Wide Deployment for
  MEIL" read as measured results; neither is. Reword as what the product does.
- **Length.** 8,439px tall on desktop and 13,764px on a phone, across more than a dozen sections; several restate the
  same three points (evidence-first, human-verified, deterministic).
- **Phone.** The eyebrow line wraps to two lines inside a pill shape; the three reassurance lines under the
  buttons wrap unevenly.
- **"Request a demo"** appears three times and opens an email with no recipient.
- **Small targets.** 11 links or buttons are under 24px on a phone.
- **Contrast.** Step numbers in pale grey on white (2.56:1); white on emerald in the illustrative screen.
- **Motion.** Entrance animations on most sections; they respect the reduced-motion setting.
- **Metadata.** Title "EmeilSG" and one description shared by every page; no social-sharing tags.
- Four unused design screenshots (6.8 MB) are publicly downloadable from `/landing/`.

### Sign in, forgot password, set password

- A gradient indigo panel and an animated two-colour border around the card: on-brand colours, but the
  moving border is decoration without a function.
- "RESET PASSWORD" is a pill-shaped, upper-case outline button beside a rectangular "Sign in" — two button
  styles on one card.
- The footer says "© MEIL · EmeilSG evidence-first BRSR reporting"; the landing footer says "EmeilSG by
  MEIL. All rights reserved." (see the compliance report).

### Shell (sidebar, top bar)

- Two ways to open the assistant on every page — "Ask" in the top bar and a floating button, which sits over
  the right edge of tables and lists.
- The Admin sidebar holds more than twenty entries in unlabelled groups; on a 900px-high screen the last groups are below
  the fold with no visible scrollbar.
- The browser icon is a red "T" (the Special theme's colour); the default theme and the landing brand are
  indigo.

### Admin Dashboard

- Clear and honest: a card with nothing to measure shows a dash and says why. No change suggested.
- 9 targets under 24px on a phone (the issue rows' icons and counts).

### Deadlines (`/admin/deadlines`)

- Title "Reporting Deadlines & Escalation Engine", section "Compliance Governance & Escalation Sweep",
  description "execute deterministic multi-tier escalations": internal vocabulary. Elsewhere the app says
  what a page is ("Submission Monitoring"). Suggest "Reporting Deadlines", "Reminders and escalation".
- Its own card, badge and colour styles (upper-case stat labels, coloured sub-lines, raw amber/emerald/rose
  classes) rather than the app's `StatusBadge` and tokens; the same "L2: CORPORATE ADMIN" chip repeats on
  every row.
- Nine columns at 1440px, with two action buttons on every row.

### Carbon credits (`/admin/carbon-credits`)

- Four pastel-tinted cards (green, purple, orange, blue) where every other page uses neutral cards.
- Shows "₹2,500 / tCO₂e — Corporate Shadow Carbon Price" when nothing was configured (see the other reports).
- Title and description are dense: "Registry inventory ledger, anti-double-counting reconciliation for SEBI
  BRSR, and Internal Carbon Pricing (ICP) CapEx analytics."

### Simulator, Forecast

- Fit all three widths now. Both use small custom buttons (11px text) alongside the standard ones; 4–5
  targets under 24px on a phone.

### Reports → Evidence bundle

- On a phone the "Generate & Download Bundle (.zip)" button is 3px wider than the screen. Not fixed.

### BRSR Mapping, BRSR Disclosures, Organization, Audit Trail

- Long pages of small inline links ("Edit", "Answer", row expanders): 100+ targets under 24px on a phone on
  Mapping and Audit Trail.
- The script flagged controls without a name on Mapping, Disclosures and Organization; these sit inside
  collapsed sections and are likely false alarms — check by hand with the sections open.

### Copy, across the app

- About 70 interface strings contain an em dash. Some are the "no value" marker in a table cell, which is
  fine; the rest join two clauses ("No approved records yet — the trend appears once a record is approved.").
  They were not counted apart. Rewriting the second kind as two sentences is a copy pass for the owner or the
  ui role, not done here.
- Page titles follow "Name · EmeilSG" except Deadlines.

## Against the twelve design rules

| Rule | Finding |
| ---- | ------- |
| 1 Gradients | Indigo-to-indigo on the sign-in panel and landing banner (brand colours); animated two-colour borders on the sign-in card and one landing card are decoration. No purple-blue template gradient. |
| 2 Pill buttons | Buttons are 6–8px radius throughout. Pills: the landing eyebrow, hero badges, "RESET PASSWORD". |
| 3, 10 Fake proof | None: no testimonials, logos, counters. Two card captions read as results (above). Sample screens are labelled. |
| 4 Vague headings | Landing headings are specific. Deadlines and Carbon credits are jargon-heavy. |
| 5 Emoji | Three, replaced. |
| 6 Em dashes | About 70 strings contain one, part of them in running copy; not changed. |
| 7 Motion and cursor | Custom cursor site-wide (owner's decision); landing entrance animations; animated borders. |
| 8 Imagery | Six infrastructure photographs with no recorded source. |
| 9 Real data and empty states | Good: explicit empty states everywhere, dashes for "nothing to measure". Exception: the carbon price. |
| 11 Consistency | Strong in the older pages; Deadlines, Carbon credits and Simulator diverge. |
| 12 Brand | Kept. Nothing was restyled. |
