# Grill: clearing the hosted test data, MEIL's Admin inputs, two sets of test data
Date: 2026-10-11

## Intent
The owner wants a clean site, MEIL's own details in the Admin's written inputs, and two folders of sample
data to run the whole flow by hand. The owner imports the users (and types their email addresses).

## Key decisions
- Decision: cleared on hosted, in one transaction, after a dry run that matched the inventory: 11 records,
  13 documents and their 13 files, every OCR / validation / verification / approval row, 17 notifications,
  the saved chat, the 0.02 test factor, the period AUG26, the org units DrillMac, Drilling and Olectra, the
  role "test", the reset code and rate-limit counters. Reason: the owner's request; each group confirmed.
  A copy of every deleted row is in `test data/_backup/` (git-ignored).
- Decision: the three accounts stay as they are (owner's choice). They could not be deleted anyway: the
  audit log refers to them and is append-only. The audit log keeps its 143 earlier entries plus one,
  `maintenance.test_data_cleared`.
- Decision: Admin inputs were entered through the app's own procedures (`brsr.saveDisclosure`,
  `reports.saveInputs`, `factors.create`) from a script acting as the one Admin account, so each is
  validated and audited as a screen entry would be. The audit entries carry no address or browser.
- Decision: only what meil.in or a document published there states was typed: 23 answers (company identity,
  activities, offices, group companies, policy links, CSR for FY 2022-23), each naming its source.
  Everything else is empty and prints "Not reported". Reason: no invented company facts.
- Decision: the published turnover (Rs 25,837.68 crore, annual return FY 2022-23) is stored under financial
  year 2022-23, not under the demo year. Reason: a figure belongs to its year; later years print "Not
  provided". Alternative considered: reuse it for 2026-27 (a false intensity figure).
- Decision: the 28 sourced factors of `.grill/audit-2-decisions.md` plus a zero factor for renewable
  electricity were entered (owner's go-ahead). PNG, steam, heat / cooling, waste and the spend-based
  activities have none.
- Decision: test data is generated by `apps/ocr-worker/scripts/make_test_data.py` and
  `apps/web/scripts/test-data-sheets.mjs` into `test data/` (git-ignored). Set 1 is August 2026, set 2 is
  September 2026 for the same projects plus new org units. Org unit names are MEIL's published units,
  companies and projects; every figure, vendor and person is invented and every page says SAMPLE.
- Decision: safety statements print counts as "1 Nos". Reason: OCR does not detect a lone digit on a line.
